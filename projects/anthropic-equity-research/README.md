# Anthropic Equity Research (pre-IPO research exercise)

**Categories:** Finance and investment research · Data analytics

A 12-slide investment research presentation prepared for the Innovation X Research Analyst Program. It evaluates Anthropic as a private company using funding-round valuations and annualized revenue in place of public share-price, EPS, and P/E data. The deck organizes an investment thesis, company and governance overview, financial and unit-economics discussion, valuation multiples, market opportunity, peer comparison, catalysts, and risks.

**What the work demonstrates:** A distinction between run-rate and recognized revenue; equity value versus enterprise value; comparisons with other AI providers; and a thesis framed around evidence that could confirm or challenge it. The appendix records sources and limitations, including the uncertainty of private-company marks and survey-based market-share estimates.

**Skills exhibited:** Equity research; private-company valuation; financial ratio analysis; competitive benchmarking; market sizing critique; scenario and risk analysis; source evaluation; executive presentation; PowerPoint data visualization.

**Scope and caveat:** This is a research exercise, not a recommendation to trade. The presentation is dated July 27, 2026. Its company, financing, filing, revenue, and market-share claims should be independently checked against primary disclosures before external investment use. No live financial model or audited statements are included in this project.
