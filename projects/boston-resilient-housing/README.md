# Sustainable Solutions for Urban Inequity in Boston

**Categories:** Sustainable development and SDGs · Data-informed policy research  
**Relevant SDGs:** SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities), SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities), and SDG 13 (Climate Action).

A team presentation by Aubrey Chihota, Jenni Ryan, Mariana Santos Velasquez, and Patrick Olaniyi. It examines unsafe, inefficient, and unaffordable housing in Roxbury, Dorchester, and Mattapan and proposes resilient housing improvements that account for health, energy costs, climate exposure, displacement, and resident participation.

The proposal prioritizes assessing housing needs using 311 reports, planning with residents, retrofitting viable homes with green repairs and weatherization, rebuilding selectively where homes are severely degraded, and maintaining the improvements. It identifies residents and public agencies among the stakeholders and frames the approach around social, economic, and environmental goals.

**Skills exhibited:** Urban policy research; housing equity analysis; stakeholder analysis; sustainability and climate resilience planning; community-centered design; problem structuring; implementation roadmap design; data-informed presentation; team collaboration.

**Sustainable finance relevance:** The proposal identifies affordable, energy-efficient housing as an investment need. It does not contain a financing structure, capital budget, or investment-return model.

**Scope:** This is a proposed approach, not evidence that retrofits or new builds were completed. The presentation attributes the project to all four team members and does not assign individual slide ownership.
