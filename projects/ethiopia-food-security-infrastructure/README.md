# Food Security and Rural Connectivity in Ethiopia

A group proposal addressing how conflict, remote geography, seasonal road closures, and limited transport infrastructure can interrupt food aid and market access. The presentation links rural road improvements with a proposed way to measure food security and strengthen distribution and local commerce.

The proposed design combines multilingual phone surveys using the FAO Food Insecurity Experience Scale, GIS links between survey responses and road improvements, baseline and endline travel-time measures, and a comparison with communities without improved infrastructure. It also sketches barcode-based distribution tracking, a USSD price and farmer connection service, mobile money, and a route-level dashboard. Stakeholder, privacy, data quality, connectivity, and community adoption risks are discussed.

**Skills exhibited:** Problem framing; monitoring and evaluation design; quasi-experimental thinking; GIS and spatial analysis concepts; survey design; data quality planning; dashboard specification; stakeholder mapping; humanitarian logistics; responsible technology design; team presentation.

**Scope:** This is a proposal presented by a group. The slides describe suggested interventions and measures; they do not document deployed software, collected survey data, completed road construction, or measured outcomes. Individual team contributions are not specified in the deck.
