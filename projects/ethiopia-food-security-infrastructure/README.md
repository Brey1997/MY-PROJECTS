# World Bank Youth Summit 2025: Food Security and Rural Connectivity in Ethiopia

**Categories:** International organization project · Data analytics · Sustainable development and SDGs  
**Relevant SDGs:** SDG 2 (Zero Hunger), SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure), and SDG 17 (Partnerships for the Goals).

A group proposal developed for the **World Bank Youth Summit 2025**. It addresses how conflict, remoteness, seasonal road closures, and limited transport infrastructure can interrupt food aid and market access in Ethiopia. The presentation links rural road improvements with a proposed framework for measuring food security and strengthening distribution and local commerce.

The proposed design combines multilingual phone surveys using the FAO Food Insecurity Experience Scale, GIS links between survey responses and road improvements, baseline and endline travel-time measures, and a comparison with communities without improved infrastructure. It also sketches barcode-based distribution tracking, a USSD price and farmer connection service, mobile money, and a route-level dashboard. Stakeholder, privacy, data quality, connectivity, and community adoption risks are discussed.

**Skills exhibited:** Monitoring and evaluation design; quasi-experimental thinking; GIS and spatial analysis concepts; survey design; data quality planning; dashboard specification; stakeholder mapping; humanitarian logistics; partnership design; responsible technology design; team presentation.

**Scope:** This is a group proposal for the summit, not a claim of World Bank employment, endorsement, funding, or implemented results. The slides describe suggested interventions and measures; they do not document deployed software, collected survey data, completed road construction, or measured outcomes. Individual team contributions are not specified in the deck.
