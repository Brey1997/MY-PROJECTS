# Machine-Learning-Augmented Sizing and Dispatch of PV-Grid-BESS Hybrid Microgrids

**Full title:** Machine-Learning-Augmented Sizing and Dispatch of PV-Grid-BESS Hybrid Microgrids to Achieve 100% Reliability at Lower LCOE: A Replicable Framework for Critical Facilities

**Authors:** Sean Tapiwa Kabera; Munashe Naphtali Mupa; Patronella Siphatisiwe Mtemeli; Peter Mangoro; Tazvitya Aubrey Chihota.  
**Publication:** *Iconic Research and Engineering Journals*, 9(8), 1085–1094 (February 2026). Paper ID 1714253.  
**Publisher links:** [Article details](https://www.irejournals.com/paper-details/1714253) · [Full text PDF](https://www.irejournals.com/formatedpaper/1714253.pdf)

## Research summary

The coauthored paper proposes a machine-learning-augmented framework for sizing and dispatching photovoltaic, grid, and battery storage microgrids serving critical facilities. It considers reliability and levelized cost of energy together, using stochastic simulation, an XGBoost surrogate for sizing, and reinforcement learning approaches for dispatch. Its illustrative hospital and data-center case studies report lower modeled LCOE while preserving the paper's stated reliability target.

**Research areas and skills reflected:** Energy systems analytics; machine learning applications; multi-objective optimization; Monte Carlo uncertainty analysis; levelized-cost analysis; grid resilience; sustainable energy research; technical research writing.

**Portfolio categories:** Published research papers · Data analytics · Sustainable finance and SDGs (energy investment economics; SDGs 7, 9, and 13).
