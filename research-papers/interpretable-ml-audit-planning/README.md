## Research summary

The coauthored paper examines interpretable machine learning for audit risk scoring in financial services. It discusses gradient boosting with SHAP explanations, comparison with traditional risk assessments, and performance measures including precision, recall, lift, and false-positive cost. It also discusses visual reporting for audit planning. The reported results point to potential gains in risk identification while acknowledging recall and false-positive limitations.

**Research areas and skills reflected:** Financial risk analytics; audit planning; interpretable machine learning; model evaluation; compliance risk; data visualization; research design; academic writing.
